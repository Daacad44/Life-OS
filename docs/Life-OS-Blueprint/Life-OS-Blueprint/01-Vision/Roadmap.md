## 1. Purpose

This is the **product-level** roadmap — the sequence of releases and the value each delivers. For the detailed **engineering** build order (phases, tasks, dependencies), see the *Development Roadmap*.

---

## 2. Roadmap Philosophy

- **Ship a usable core first**, then layer intelligence and expansion.
- Each release must stand on its own as a valuable product.
- The AI is added on top of a working app, never before it.
- Expansion features come only after the core proves itself.

---

## 3. Release Milestones

| Release | Theme | Outcome |
|---------|-------|---------|
| **V1** | Core Product | A usable, unified productivity system |
| **V2** | Intelligence | The AI coach and memory make it smart |
| **V3** | Life Platform | Full life coverage: health, finance, learning, business |
| **V4** | Ecosystem | Voice, automation, community, extensibility |

---

## 4. V1 — Core Product

**Goal:** A unified productivity system people use daily.

Includes: Auth, Dashboard, Task Manager, Daily Planner, Goal Engine, Calendar, Habit System.

**Value:** Replaces several separate apps with one clean, connected system — even before AI.

---

## 5. V2 — Intelligence

**Goal:** Make Life OS genuinely smart.

Includes: AI Memory, AI Personal Coach, AI Goal/Habit intelligence, AI Reflection, Weekly Review, Life Analytics, Notifications Intelligence.

**Value:** The system now remembers, coaches, and gives insight — the core differentiator goes live.

---

## 6. V3 — Life Platform

**Goal:** Cover the whole of life, not just tasks.

Includes: Finance Planner, Health Tracker, Study Planner, Career Planner, Second Brain, AI Search, Focus Mode, Business Workspace.

**Value:** Life OS becomes the single home for every area of a user's life.

---

## 7. V4 — Ecosystem

**Goal:** A proactive companion and a platform.

Includes: Voice Assistant, Workflow Automation, Smart Recommendations, Gamification, Community.

**Value:** Life OS acts on its own, adapts to the user, and grows a community around shared progress.
